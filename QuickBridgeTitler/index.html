About Quick Bridge Titler
Quick Bridge Titler
Version: 9.8.7
Copyright: Md. Toufiquzzaman
What it is. Quick Bridge Titler is a Premiere Pro panel for making animated text titles without leaving Premiere. You type your text, choose a font and a style, pick one of the ready-made animations, and press one button. It also does lower thirds, intros, rolling and crawling (news ticker) titles, and batches of many titles at once.
How it works. The panel does not draw the final title itself. It sends your settings to After Effects, which builds the animated composition and saves it as a project (.aep). That composition is then imported into your Premiere project and linked through Dynamic Link, so you can edit it again at any time and Premiere shows the change.
What you control. The Text & Style tab holds the text, font, colours, outline, shadow, background box and shapes. The Output options tab holds the animation, timing, custom positioning, rolling and crawling, and the composition size. The small previews are only a fast approximation: After Effects draws the real result.
Saving your work. Preset management keeps everything you save in one list with a tag filter: Text style presets, Lower third and Intro & outro templates, Roll & crawl presets and Saved titles (the text with its whole look). Saved Themes keep the look of the panel. The Batch Creator builds up to 20 titles in one go, each as its own After Effects project, and a title can have its own exception settings.
What is necessary. Premiere Pro and After Effects must both be installed and be the same version (for example CC2015 of both, otherwise importing into Premiere will not work). Keep After Effects open in the background while you create titles, and switch on the panel debug mode once with the file in the install folder (Windows: the .reg file, Mac: the .command file) so Premiere will load the panel.
