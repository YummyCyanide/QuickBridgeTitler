QUICK BRIDGE TITLER  -  Premiere Pro CC 2015 panel
================================================
Creates animated-text After Effects projects and links them into your Premiere
project (Dynamic Link) from one panel. Keep After Effects CC 2015 open in the
background.

ONE-TIME SETUP
--------------
1. Enable unsigned panels (needed once):
   Windows: double-click  install\enable_debug_mode_win.reg  and accept.
   Mac:     run  install/enable_debug_mode_mac.command  (right-click > Open).

2. Copy this whole "QuickBridgeTitler" folder to:
   Windows: C:\Users\<YOU>\AppData\Roaming\Adobe\CEP\extensions\
   Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/
   (create the CEP\extensions folders if they don't exist)

3. In After Effects: Edit > Preferences > General >
   tick "Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network".

4. Restart Premiere Pro, then open:  Window > Extensions > Quick Bridge Titler

DAILY USE
---------
1. Open After Effects (an empty project is best - see note below).
2. In Premiere: type your text, choose font/size/style/colors (click a color
   swatch to open the color picker), click
   "Create & Link to Premiere".
3. The panel asks After Effects to build the comp, saves the .aep (default:
   an "Ae Linked Comp" folder next to your Premiere project) and imports
   the comp into the "Ae Linked Comp" bin. Optionally it also places the comp on
   the timeline (see "Import to the Current Position of Track No" below).
4. To edit later: right-click the clip > Edit Original (opens in AE).
